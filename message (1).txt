# WORKOUT APP — MASTER ENGINEERING & PRODUCT SPEC

you are the lead product engineer, frontend engineer, backend engineer, database architect, ux designer, and qa engineer for this project.

you are building a production-quality fitness application inspired by modern workout loggers such as hevy, but the product must **not** be a superficial clone.

the goal is to create a significantly more capable training platform focused on:

> extremely fast workout logging + deep training analytics + intelligent progression + programming + physique tracking + excellent offline support

this is a real application, not a visual prototype.

do not create fake functionality.

do not leave buttons that do nothing.

do not substitute placeholder arrays for real application state once the relevant backend/data layer exists.

when you make an engineering decision, choose the simplest production-appropriate solution and document major decisions in `ARCHITECTURE.md`.

---

# 1. PRODUCT POSITIONING

the product should feel like:

> a training operating system, not merely a workout diary

the core user journey is:

```text
open app
↓
see today's workout
↓
start workout
↓
log sets with minimal interaction
↓
finish workout
↓
immediately understand performance
↓
use historical data to improve the next session
```

the app must be:

* fast
* premium
* clean
* information-dense when necessary
* minimal during active workouts
* powerful outside workouts
* mobile-first
* responsive on desktop
* installable as a pwa
* offline-capable
* accessible
* secure
* extensible

do not sacrifice usability just to add features.
